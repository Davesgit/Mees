# Mees Illustration Library v1

Status: approved direction, production candidates
Date: 2026-09-30

## Core environmental family
The first four environment concepts are approved as the visual direction:
1. Forest discovery path — warm woodland, creek/bridge, discovery cues.
2. Mountains and lake — open alpine landscape, exploration/world orientation.
3. Coast and lighthouse — sea, dunes/coast, navigation/story context.
4. Discovery study — warm nature-connected indoor learning/project space.

## Style rules
- Environments belong to the same visual world as Mees but do not use a sticker outline.
- Rich, warm and adventurous, with soft dimensional illustration rather than flat corporate vectors.
- No UI text, buttons or mascot baked into scene masters.
- Keep composition useful for responsive UI overlays and crops.
- Avoid excessive decorative noise behind primary learning content.
- The world may be richer on Home/Discover than in Focus Mode.

## Age progression
Do not create separate universes per grade.
- Group 3–4: richer crop, more story/detail, Mees more prominent.
- Group 5–6: balanced environment and UI.
- Group 7–8: calmer crop, more negative space, fewer playful foreground details.
The underlying scene family stays recognizably Mees.

## Required delivery variants per approved scene
- master landscape image
- wide/hero crop
- card crop
- mobile crop
Where possible derive crops from the same approved master instead of regenerating the scene.
