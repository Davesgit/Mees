# Mees Character Masters

Status: APPROVED CHARACTER SPECIFICATION
Date: 2026-09-30

This specification freezes the identity of Mees before production variants are created.

## Canonical forms

### MEES-MASCOT-BASE-001 — Full-body Mees
Use for learning sessions, explanations, scenes, poses, adventures and contextual illustrations.

Identity:
- rounded/chubby blue bird;
- three-lobed rounded crest;
- large friendly glossy eyes;
- dark-blue simple eyebrows;
- cream-white face transition and belly;
- warm orange/yellow beak;
- small orange feet;
- soft layered blue wings and short tail;
- friendly, curious default expression;
- polished soft 3D/cartoon illustration treatment.

Default pose:
- upright and relaxed;
- wings naturally lowered/folded;
- no prop;
- no permanent accessory;
- no white outline in the clean master.

Raised wings are an action/welcome pose, not the canonical neutral pose.

### MEES-BRAND-HEAD-001 — Compact Mees
Use for horizontal logo, app icon, favicon, compact avatar and small UI contexts.

Identity:
- same character and facial proportions as Full-body Mees;
- compact rounded head/body crop;
- no legs or feet;
- no contextual props;
- must remain recognizable at small sizes.

Compact Mees is not a separate or younger character.

## Anatomy invariants
All variants must preserve:
- crest silhouette;
- eye character/proportions;
- blue-to-cream facial transition;
- beak family and orange/yellow color;
- rounded body language;
- wing/feather visual language.

Never add a permanent yellow, beige or brown object on the back, neck or behind the head. Such elements in generated concepts are artifacts unless explicitly required as a contextual prop.

## Props
Props are encouraged in contextual variants, including backpack, cap, map, book, pointer, magnifying glass, notebook and compass. Props must be removable and meaningful to the scene. They never become part of canonical anatomy.

## Outline
The clean transparent master is canonical.

A soft white Mees Outline is an optional presentation treatment for busy/colored backgrounds or special sticker-like contexts. It is not mandatory and must not be baked into every asset.

## Expression range
Mees may be curious, thinking, calm, helping, explaining, focused, happy, proud, surprised, enthusiastic or resting. Avoid making every state maximally excited. Calm and concentration are first-class expressions.

## Age progression
The character identity does not change between groups 3–8. What changes is frequency, scale, scene richness and pose intensity:
- group 3–4: more prominent and narrative;
- group 5–6: balanced;
- group 7–8: more selective and calmer.

## Production QA checklist
A new mascot asset can be approved only if:
1. it clearly depicts the canonical Mees;
2. anatomy matches this specification;
3. no accidental permanent accessory appears;
4. expression fits the intended context;
5. transparency/background behavior is correct;
6. outline treatment follows context;
7. it remains legible at intended display size;
8. its stable asset ID is registered in ASSET-LIBRARY.md.
